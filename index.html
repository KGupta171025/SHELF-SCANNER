Privacy Policy

×

1. Local Storage Privacy: ShelfScanner stores your reading lists, preferences, and API keys exclusively inside your browser's private sandbox (localStorage).

2. Direct API Mode: In Direct Gemini API mode, your requests and bookshelf images are transmitted directly from your browser to Google's official Gemini endpoint. No intermediate servers ever store your photos or API keys.

3. Zero Tracking: We do not track, collect, sell, or profile your reading habits or personal information.
